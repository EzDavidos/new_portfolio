FAQ

Common questions

How long will the development take?

Rough guide: a Telegram bot — about 7 days, a Mini App — about 14 days, a website with CRM — from 30 days.

Timelines depend on the scope of the task.

How does the work start?

We discuss your task over chat or a call. We look at it from different angles, and then I propose a solution and name the price and the timeline. I can also look at a product you already have: it may be enough to improve it.

How does payment work, and can the price go up?

I work with prepayment: 50% before the work starts and 50% after launch. For a large project we agree on the payment schedule separately. The agreed price does not change as long as the work stays within the agreed scope. New features and changes after the start are paid separately.

Who owns the code, the domain and the database?

You do. We open the accounts in your name from the start: the repository, hosting, domain and database stay under your control.

What happens after the product launches?

For the first week I watch closely how it runs. After that my code is under warranty for a year. If you want me to keep an eye on the product and keep developing it, there is monthly support.

What are your working hours?

I am available from 8:00 to 22:00 (UTC+7) and try to reply within 40 minutes.

Shall we talk about your project?

Tell me what eats the most of your time. I will go through the process, propose a solution and name the timeline and price before any work starts.
